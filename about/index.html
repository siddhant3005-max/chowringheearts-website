About Chowringhee Arts

Horn, bone and shell, made by hand in Kolkata

We are an export house on Park Street, Kolkata. For 10 years our karigars have made homeware in buffalo horn, bone inlay, mother-of-pearl, seashell and resin for buyers abroad.

Our story

Real materials, real hands.

Chowringhee Arts has been in business for 10 years. From our office at 75C Park Street, in the heart of Kolkata, we make and export handmade homeware for importers, wholesalers, retail chains, hotel suppliers and buying agents.

The range has grown to more than 4,000 designs: bowls, trays, dishes, boxes, cutlery and serveware, table accessories, desk pieces, combs and small furniture.

We do not sell retail. Buyers tell us what they need, and we send photos, sizes and ex-works prices for the pieces that fit.

Our people

Made by our karigars.

Everything we sell is made by hand. Our pieces are made by many karigars, skilled artisans, and women are a big part of our team.

Horn is cut, shaped and polished by hand until its natural grain shows. Bone inlay is set one small piece at a time. Mother-of-pearl is carved into dishes, birds and flowers. It is patient work, and no two pieces are exactly alike.

Our materials

Horn and bone that would otherwise go to waste.

Our buffalo horn and bone are by-products of cattle farming. No animal is harmed for our products.

We use real buffalo horn, never faux. Every horn piece has its own grain, and every shell its own shimmer.

We also work in mother-of-pearl, seashell and resin, and finish many pieces with brass details.
