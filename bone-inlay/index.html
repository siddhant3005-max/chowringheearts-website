The craft

How bone inlay is made

The bone we use is a by-product of cattle farming: material that would otherwise go to waste. No animal is harmed for our products.

Our karigars cut the bone into small pieces and set them by hand, one piece at a time, into the pattern.

Because every piece is set by hand, small differences in the pattern from one box or tray to the next are normal.
