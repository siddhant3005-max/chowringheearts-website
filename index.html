Why Chowringhee Arts

Real materials, real hands.

Chowringhee Arts is an export house on Park Street, in the heart of Kolkata. We work with karigars — skilled artisans — who make home décor, gifting and serveware in buffalo horn, bone inlay and mother-of-pearl for importers, wholesalers and independent stores abroad.

Every piece starts with material that would otherwise go to waste, and ends as something made entirely by hand.

  1. 01The material

    A second life for horn and bone

    Our buffalo horn and bone are by-products of cattle farming — material that would otherwise go to waste. Our karigars give it a second life as bowls, trays, boxes and serveware.

    No animal is harmed for our products.

  2. Bone inlay, set by hand

    02The craft

    Made by hand, piece by piece

    Horn is cut, shaped and polished by hand until its natural grain shows. Bone inlay is set one small piece at a time. Mother-of-pearl is carved into dishes, birds and flowers.

    It is patient work, and you can see it in the finish.

  3. Three horn bowls — each one different

    03The result

    No two alike

    Natural horn has its own grain and every shell has its own shimmer, so no two pieces are exactly the same.

    Your customers get something made by hand, with a story they can tell — not something off a production line.

Made by hand in India, from material that would otherwise go to waste.

What we work in

Horn, bone & shell.

Three natural materials, each worked by hand. Real pieces from our range — not stock photos.

Buffalo horn

Real buffalo horn, a by-product of cattle farming, polished to bring out its grain. Every piece is different; many are finished with brass.

Bone inlay

Bone, also a by-product of cattle farming, set by hand one piece at a time into patterns — trays, boxes and more.

Mother-of-pearl & shell

The natural shimmer of shell — cut, carved into birds and flowers, and inlaid into dishes and serveware.
