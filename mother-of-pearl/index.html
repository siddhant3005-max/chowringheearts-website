The craft

How mother-of-pearl is made and finished

Mother-of-pearl is the shimmering inner layer of the shell. Our karigars cut it, carve it into birds and flowers, and inlay it into dishes and serveware.

Some dishes are engraved, some are set with stones and some are trimmed with metal. Every piece is finished by hand.

Every shell has its own shimmer and colour, so no two pieces are exactly alike. Expect small differences across an order.

FAQ

More detail on payment, samples and fairs is on how we trade.

What is the lead time?

Orders are ready 30 to 45 days after we receive the 50% advance. The balance is due before dispatch.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Prices get better from 100 pieces and again from 300 pieces per design. Tell us the quantities you have in mind and we will quote for those.

Can I order samples?

Yes. Samples are made on request and charged at the piece price.

Can you make custom sizes, colours or finishes?

Yes. Sizes, finishes and trims are made to your specification. Send a drawing, photo or reference piece with the quantity.

How are the pieces packed?

Tell us how your market needs goods packed and labelled when you enquire, and we will confirm it with the quote.

Where does the shell come from?

Ask us about the shell used in the pieces you are interested in and we will tell you for those pieces.

Do you send a price list or catalogue?

We quote for the pieces you ask about rather than sending a full catalogue: tell us what interests you and we send photos, sizes and ex-works Kolkata prices for those.
