Step by step

How an order works.

We start from your requirement, so you only see the pieces that fit.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you need

    The pieces you are interested in, rough quantities per design and your market. Photos from this site or your own references are fine.

  2. 2

    Photos, sizes and prices

    We reply with photos, sizes and ex-works Kolkata prices in US dollars for those pieces. We do not send full catalogues or price lists.

  3. 3

    Samples

    If you want to see a piece first, we make samples on request, charged at the piece price.

  4. 4

    Order and advance

    Confirm the pieces, quantities and any custom sizes, colours or finishes. We start on receipt of a 50% advance.

  5. 5

    Production

    Everything is made by hand by our karigars. Orders are ready 30 to 45 days after the advance.

  6. 6

    Balance and dispatch

    The balance is paid before dispatch. Goods are sold ex-works Kolkata.

Questions buyers ask

FAQ

Do you sell to consumers?

No. We sell to the trade only: importers, wholesalers, retail chains, hotel suppliers and buying agents.

Do you send a price list or catalogue?

No. We do not publish prices or send full catalogues. Tell us the pieces you are interested in and we send photos, sizes and ex-works Kolkata prices for those.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Prices get better from 100 pieces and again from 300 pieces per design. Tell us the quantities you have in mind and we will quote for those.

Can I order samples?

Yes. Samples are made on request and charged at the piece price.

What are the payment terms?

50% advance with the order, and the balance before dispatch.

How long does an order take?

Orders are ready 30 to 45 days after we receive the advance.

Where are prices quoted?

Ex-works Kolkata, in US dollars.

Can I see your pieces in person?

Yes. We exhibit at IHGF Delhi Fair. The next edition is IHGF Spring 2027, 15 to 19 March 2027, at India Expo Centre and Mart, Greater Noida. Write to us for our stand number before the fair.
